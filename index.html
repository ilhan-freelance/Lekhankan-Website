HERITAGE • DISCIPLINE • PRECISION

Bringing Traditional Financial Discipline to Modern Cloud Accounting

In Sanskrit and Hindi, "Lekhankan" stands for the art and science of systematic record-keeping and financial accounting. We carry forward this centuries-old legacy of precision into the digital era, helping businesses and CPA firms across the United States and Canada streamline their back-office finance operations.

By deploying dedicated offshore accounting teams managed by seasoned Chartered Accountants, we eliminate financial friction, lower operational costs by up to 60%, and deliver real-time financial clarity to business leaders.
