"Lekhankan" is derived from the Sanskrit and Hindi word for accounting and systematic record-keeping. It reflects our commitment to precision, transparency, and disciplined financial management. While our name reflects our heritage, our services are built for modern businesses across North America using world-class cloud accounting technology.

ABOUT LEKHANKAN

Lekhankan is an Accounting Knowledge Process Outsourcing (KPO) company built on the philosophy of combining accounting expertise with modern technology.

We help businesses simplify accounting operations, improve reporting accuracy, and create scalable finance functions through dedicated offshore accounting teams.

Our goal is not only to maintain books but to build long-term financial partnerships that create measurable business value.
