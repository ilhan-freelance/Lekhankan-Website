OUR SERVICES 
Offshore Bookkeeping Services 
Daily Transaction Recording 
We maintain accurate records of your day-to-day financial transactions, including income, 
expenses, deposits, and payments. Our team categorizes transactions according to your Chart of 
Accounts and accounting policies, ensuring your books remain current, organized, and ready for 
reconciliation, reporting, tax preparation, and informed business decision-making. 
Bank Reconciliation 
We reconcile your bank accounts regularly by comparing accounting records with bank 
statements and investigating discrepancies. Our process identifies missing transactions, 
duplicates, timing differences, bank charges, and unexplained balances, helping maintain 
accurate cash positions and ensuring your financial statements reflect reliable and up-to-date 
banking information. 
Credit Card Reconciliation 
We reconcile business credit card accounts against monthly statements, reviewing purchases, 
refunds, payments, fees, and outstanding balances. Our accounting professionals identify 
duplicate or incorrectly categorized transactions and ensure every card activity is properly 
recorded, supported by documentation, and reflected accurately in your financial records. 
General Ledger Maintenance 
Our team maintains a clean and organized General Ledger by accurately recording transactions, 
reviewing account classifications, and identifying unusual or incorrect entries. Regular General 
Ledger reviews help ensure financial information remains reliable and provide a strong 
foundation for monthly closing, financial reporting, tax preparation, and management analysis. 
Chart of Accounts Management 
We help maintain a structured Chart of Accounts aligned with your business model, reporting 
requirements, and accounting policies. Our team reviews account classifications, identifies 
redundant or incorrectly used accounts, and maintains consistency across transactions, enabling 
clearer financial reporting and more meaningful analysis of revenue, expenses, assets, liabilities, 
and profitability. 
Journal Entries 
Our accounting professionals prepare and post appropriate journal entries for accruals, 
prepayments, depreciation, reclassifications, payroll adjustments, loan activity, corrections, and 
other accounting requirements. Each entry is reviewed for proper account classification and 
supporting documentation, helping ensure your financial statements accurately represent the 
underlying business activity. 
Month-End Bookkeeping 
We manage the complete month-end bookkeeping cycle, including transaction review, 
reconciliations, adjustments, account analysis, and financial statement preparation. Our 
structured closing process helps ensure your books are complete and accurate each month, 
enabling management to receive timely financial information and identify trends, issues, and 
opportunities. 
Historical Bookkeeping Cleanup 
If your books are outdated, inconsistent, or unreconciled, our team can perform historical 
bookkeeping cleanup. We review prior transactions, reconcile accounts, correct classifications, 
remove duplicates, address outstanding items, and organize financial records, helping bring your 
accounting system to an accurate and reliable position.
